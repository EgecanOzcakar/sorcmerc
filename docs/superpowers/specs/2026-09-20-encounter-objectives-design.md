# Encounter objectives — the same fight, asked a different question

Sub-project 1 of the 2026-09-20 content batch (objectives → landmarks → threat
clocks and reclaiming → faction ladder and renown → callings with party
relations → downtime → the lodge). Every fight in the game today is "kill
everyone" (`Combat.outcome()`: Victory when the foe team is out, Defeat when
the party is). This adds five objectives that ride in the encounter spec and
change what the fight is *for* while reusing the board, the roster generator,
the AI and the dice unchanged. It is the cheapest variety per unit of work in
the batch because the fight is the game.

## 0. Scope

**In:** the objective contract on the spec; five kinds (hold, rescue,
breakout, hunt, escort); the two bystander tokens they need (captive, carter);
the win/fail rules inside `core/combat.gd`; one AI rule (the quarry runs); the
autopilot learning one rule per kind so difficulty can be measured; the brief,
the HUD line and the spoils row; the reward rule; **one world source per
kind** so each is reachable in normal play; co-op carrying the objective
through the existing setup message; tests.

**Out:** M9 scripted story fights (this is the seam they will use — an
objective is a key on the spec, so a pack's `fights` block gets it for free
later); a moving carter or a captive walked off the board; foes that reason
about objectives beyond the two rules below; raids (C1 supplies the second
source for hold); drawn interiors.

**Untouched behaviour:** a spec without `objective` is today's fight exactly.
Every existing test and the 200-seed scaler sweep must pass unchanged.

## 1. The contract

```gdscript
spec["objective"] = {"kind": "hold", "rounds": 5, "waves": [[{"id": "snik", "count": 2}]]}
```

`Encounter.build()` copies `spec.get("objective", {})` onto
`Combat.objective`. Absent or empty means `rout`, the current rule. The
objective owns three things and nothing else:

| owns | where |
|---|---|
| **extra tokens** placed at build time | `Encounter.build()` |
| **over / outcome** — when the fight ends and what it says | `Combat.is_over()`, `Combat.outcome()`, plus one `objective_done: bool` and `objective_failed: bool` |
| **a round hook** — what happens at the top of a round | `Combat.end_turn()` where `round_num` advances, calling `_objective_round()` |

Everything downstream (`Encounter.resolve_outcome()`, the world's `_bank()`,
the spoils page, quests) reads `outcome`, `objective_done` and the kind. No
system outside `combat.gd`/`encounter.gd` has to know the rules of any kind.

Outcomes stay two-valued. An objective can **end** a fight in Victory with
foes still standing (the gate held, the party got out, the quarry fell), and
it can **fail** without ending it (the captive killed, the wagon burnt, the
quarry gone) — the fight then runs on to rout as today and `objective_done`
stays false. "Victory, objective failed" is a real result and the spoils page
says so.

## 2. The five kinds

Defaults are starting points; §8 says how they get tuned.

### hold — "Hold the passage for five rounds."
- **Setup.** Ordinary deployment. `rounds` (default 5). `waves`: a list of
  rosters; wave *i* spawns at the top of round *i + 2* at the far (high-q) foe
  spots (`Encounter._foe_spots`, the `far` bucket), so reinforcements come
  from the direction the foes did. Waves are generated by the same
  `Scaler.roster_for()` call as the opening roster at ~half its power, and
  their ids and counts sit in the spec, so lockstep peers spawn identical
  waves.
- **Win.** The top of round `rounds + 1` with any hero conscious → Victory,
  foes standing or not ("the gate is barred behind you"). Rout also wins.
- **Fail.** Party out → Defeat, as today.
- **HUD.** "Hold — round 3 of 5". Wave arrival goes to the log.

### rescue — "Get to the captive before the fourth round ends."
- **Setup.** A **captive** bystander (§3) placed on the free hex farthest from
  every party start among the foe spots. `deadline` (default 4).
- **Freeing.** A conscious hero ending a move or a turn adjacent to the
  captive frees it (`freed` status, one log line). No action cost — reaching
  it is the cost.
- **Fail.** Top of round `deadline + 1`, not freed → the captive is killed
  (`hp = 0`, marked dead), `objective_failed`. The fight continues.
- **Win.** Rout, as today; `objective_done` if freed.
- **Foes never attack the captive.** `AI._foe_turn()`'s target list excludes
  `captive`; the executioner rule above is the only thing that harms it. A
  fireball still can — a hero's area spell that catches it is a hero's
  problem, and `PartyOpinion.friendly_fire` will care later.
- **HUD.** "Captive — 2 rounds left" / "Captive — freed".

### breakout — "You are surrounded. Get everyone to the road."
- **Setup.** Party deploys in the board's middle band (the deploy screen's
  legal hexes shift to the central third of q); foes spawn at *both* ends
  (`near` and `far` buckets alternate). The **exit** is the four open hexes
  nearest the high-q edge, painted on the board like a move field.
- **Win.** End of any turn on which every conscious hero stands on an exit hex
  → Victory. Downed heroes are carried (the retreat narrative already says
  "they come to at…"). Rout also wins, and pays more — the card says so.
- **Fail.** Party out → Defeat.
- **HUD.** "Road — 2 of 4 heroes there".

### hunt — "Kill the quarry before it reaches the treeline."
- **Setup.** The roster's strongest monster (`Power.estimate`) is the
  **quarry** (`quarry` status, spawned at the farthest foe spot). The far edge
  is its escape.
- **AI rule (the one new foe behaviour).** On its turn, if a conscious hero is
  within 3 hexes it fights as normal (cornered); otherwise it moves with
  `AI._away(pcs)` biased toward the high-q edge and uses no action. Ending a
  turn on a far-edge perimeter hex → **escaped**: removed from the board, not
  dead, `objective_failed`.
- **Win.** Quarry dead → the escort scatters: fight over, Victory, kills paid
  for the dead only. If it escaped, the fight runs to rout as today.
- **HUD.** "Quarry — 6 hexes from the treeline".

### escort — "The carter has to live."
- **Setup.** A **carter** bystander (§3) on the party start nearest the
  centre; the heroes deploy around it.
- **AI.** Nothing new. `_foe_turn()` already targets the lowest-HP conscious
  party-team combatant in reach, which is the carter whenever a foe gets
  there. The puzzle is body-blocking.
- **Fail.** Carter dead → `objective_failed`, fight continues.
- **Win.** Rout; `objective_done` if the carter lives.
- **HUD.** "Carter — 9 HP".

## 3. Bystanders

A bystander is a `Combatant` on team `party` with `sheet == null` (the shape
a summon already has) and a `bystander` status. It is:

- **out of the initiative order** — `Combat.new()` builds `order` from
  combatants without `bystander`, so it never has a turn and the co-op turn
  loops never see it;
- **not a hero** — `_mine()`/ownership, the deploy list, the order strip, the
  hero menu, hover and `Party.to_combatants` all skip it; it is never written
  back by `Adapter.write_back_all` (a null sheet already is not);
- **not a death** — `resolve_outcome()` excludes it from `deaths` and
  `downed` the way a faded summon is excluded;
- **drawn** — a token with its own tint and a label, using the summon token
  path in `main.gd`.

Captive: AC 10, `hp = 4`, `captive` status (untargetable by the AI, cannot be
moved by shove). Carter: AC 11, `hp = 6 + 2 × party level`.

## 4. Rewards

`Encounter.resolve_outcome()` adds `"objective": {"kind": k, "done": bool}`
and applies one rule, stated once: **an objective done pays half the whole
roster's worth in XP on top of the kills** — dead or standing, because holding
against them, slipping past them, or dropping their leader is the deed. This
is the batch's milestone-XP rule ("XP for deeds, not only kills") in its first
concrete form. Gold and loot stay kills-only; a foe you did not kill did not
drop anything.

The world layer pays the *reason* for the objective through the source that
set it (§5): a job's reward, a quest's completion, faction opinion.

## 5. Where each objective comes from

One source per kind, so every objective is reachable in ordinary play from
this PR; later sub-projects add more (raids → hold, callings → rescue).

| kind | source | wiring |
|---|---|---|
| **hold** | a site room kind, **the gate**: "Hold the passage while the way behind is barred" | `Site.COMBAT_ROOMS` gains a `hold` room; `site.gd` sets the objective on the room's spec; waves drawn from the site's faction pool |
| **rescue** | a site room kind, **the pens**, and a board job kind **`rescue`** ("Bring back the miller's boy from the Ash Warren") | `Quest.KINDS` gains `rescue` with `target_lair_id`; posting offers it for a discovered, uncleared lair; the pens room appears in that lair and `Quest.record_rescued(party, lair_id)` completes it on `objective_done` |
| **breakout** | a **camp ambush** where the watch fails | `world.gd`'s camp path already passes `forced_ambush`; it now also sets `objective = breakout` with a roster at the tier's *hard* power — a fight you are not meant to win by standing |
| **hunt** | a **`hunt_party` job** met on the road | `_launch_combat()`: if a live `hunt_party` quest targets this band, the spec gets `hunt`; the band's strongest monster is the quarry; `record_party_defeated` fires only on `objective_done`. A band whose chief got away is not broken: on Victory-with-escape it is **not** erased from the map — it is moved off a day's march and marked `_slipped`, and the job stays open, so the quarry can be run down again |
| **escort** | a **`deliver_goods` job** | while a delivery is active, every road fight is an escort; the carter dying fails the delivery (`Quest.fail(party, id)` — a new terminal state with a log line; the board can post it again) |

The approach card and the site's room card carry the objective's one-line
brief as their title line, so the player reads the question before the board
comes up.

## 6. UI

- **Deploy screen:** the brief as the banner ("Hold the passage — five
  rounds. Reinforcements will come from the tunnel."). Breakout's deploy field
  is the middle band; its exit hexes are painted from the first frame.
- **HUD:** one line under the round counter, per kind as listed in §2,
  refreshed in `_refresh()`.
- **Log:** wave arrival, the captive freed / killed, the quarry cornered /
  escaped, the carter hit, the exit reached — each one line, in the fight's
  own voice.
- **Spoils page:** a row "Objective — Held the gate" / "Objective — the
  captive was killed on round 5", above XP, with the bonus XP shown as its own
  number.
- **Co-op:** the objective travels in `Coop.setup_for()`'s spec; bystanders
  are built from the seed on both peers; the HUD line is derived state, so
  nothing new crosses the wire. Bystanders are never owned by anyone.

## 7. The autopilot learns one rule per kind

`AI._party_auto()` is what every sweep and both drive robots run the party
with, and it does not know what a captive is. One movement preference per
kind, nothing cleverer: rescue — the hero nearest the captive moves toward it
before acting; breakout — every hero moves toward the nearest exit hex, and
attacks only if already there or adjacent to a foe; escort — a hero more than
2 hexes from the carter closes to it before attacking; hunt — heroes prefer
the quarry as a target when in reach; hold — no rule (holding is fighting).
This is not to make the robots good; it is so §8 measures a party that is
*trying*, which is the only number that means anything.

## 8. Testing, and the numbers that gate the merge

`tests/test_objectives.gd`, seeded, headless, on the autopilot:

- **The contract:** a spec without `objective` produces a `Combat` whose
  `order`, `outcome()` and `resolve_outcome()` are unchanged — asserted by
  running the existing Sunken Shrine seeds through both paths and comparing
  the outcome dictionaries.
- **Per kind, the rule:** hold ends at the top of round `rounds + 1` with foes
  alive and says Victory; a wave spawns on the round it is due, at a far spot;
  the captive dies at the deadline if unfreed and is untouched by the foe AI
  before it; freeing needs adjacency and nothing else; breakout ends when the
  last conscious hero reaches the exit and not before; the quarry runs when
  nobody is within 3 and fights when someone is, and escapes only from a
  far-edge hex; the carter is attacked by an adjacent foe before a healthier
  hero is; a dead bystander is in neither `deaths` nor `downed`.
- **Bystanders never act:** an invariant added to `drive_random.gd`'s frame
  checks — no `bystander` is ever `current()`.
- **The number:** 80 seeds per kind at `normal`, autopilot with §7's rules.
  The kind's *done* rate must land in **40–75%** — an objective that is nearly
  free is a modifier, one that is nearly impossible is a trap. Tuned by the
  kind's own knob (rounds, deadline, carter HP, wave power, quarry threshold),
  **never by the roster** — the tier's win rate is `scaler.gd`'s promise and
  this PR does not touch it. The measured grid goes in the file header, the
  way `site.gd`'s and `regions.gd`'s do.
- **Co-op:** `tests/test_coop.gd` gains a lockstep case per kind — both peers
  replay a hunt and an escort from the same setup to the same hash.

## 10. Open, decided here unless overruled

- A hero downed on an exit hex counts as carried, not as on the exit; the
  conscious heroes must all be there.
- The quarry that escapes takes its loot with it; nothing is rolled for it.
- `rescue` jobs pay gold and +opinion with the lair's nearest settlement; the
  freed captive is narrative (a line on the spoils page), not a token that
  follows the party home. A callings sub-project may later make one a person.
- A breakout at camp does not roll the `_hp_toll` invariant away: the fight
  is a fight and may drop anyone, as any camp ambush already may.
