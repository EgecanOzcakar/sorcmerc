## The Far Deeps and a lair for every people — four of the owner's calls on the far end of the map (2026-09-25)

Four of the owner's calls on the design audit (docs/audit-game-design.md,
"The owner's calls"): §8.3 lairs for every home faction, §8.4 more CR 10+
monsters and bands for the Deeps' own peoples, and §5.4's split of the Far
Deeps into sub-bands. They land together because they are one complaint from
three directions: the far end of the map is where the audit estimated most of a
run is spent, and it was the thinnest part of the game.

**A lair for every people (§8.3).** Every map the game builds held the same five
lairs — a goblin warren, a giant hold, two graveyards and a dragon's cave — so
of the fifteen factions `core/site.gd` has a boss for, four could ever be met at
the bottom of one, and the cult's voice, the one enemy that casts from real
slots, stood at the bottom of a lair no map had. A new pass,
`core/world_homes.gd`, runs last in each builder (after the landmarks, before
the roads fill) and places one lair in the home country of every faction in
`Scaler.FACTIONS` that has a home in `Regions.HOMES` and no lair there yet:
Cutthroat Hollow (bandit), The Tangle (beast), The Scale Pits (kobold), The
Tusk Camp (orc), The Boneyard (gnoll), The Quiet Chapel (cultist), The Roost
(monstrosity), The Restless Hill (elemental), The Silent Foundry (construct)
and The Green Hollow (fey). Soldier has no home and gets none. Placement is the
procedural builder's own shape — a rejection sampler in the home band's ring,
clear of towns, lairs, landmarks and water — inside the map's measured extent,
so it cannot move a seam. Each builder passes a fixed seed (the small map 41,
the large 43, a generated map its seed + 3), so the same map is the same map,
and a generated map's first five lairs, lake, woods and landmarks are
byte-for-byte what the seed always made: the five named lairs are still drawn
from their faction's whole country (`Regions.country_fracs`), and the new pass
draws from its own stream after everything else. Both hand-placed maps had
their zombie graveyard a hair past the frontier's seam (frac 0.87), which left
the undead homeless and would have dug them a second graveyard; it moved twenty
units in on each. A save of a built map from before this pass gets the lairs it
lacks when it loads (`WorldHomes.backfill`, raid clocks starting then, not at
day 0), and every save since carries `"origin": {..., "homes": true}` so it is
never filled twice. A pack's map is its author's and is never filled.

A lair with no model of its own used to draw nothing — `Lairs3D._build`
returned null for any id outside the five, so a pack's lairs showed a name over
bare ground. Every lair now draws: an unknown id gets its faction's kit
(`LairKit.build_for`), one of the five diorama shapes with the faction's own
palette, seeded by the lair's id — a stockade in orange hide for the kobolds, a
dark ruin with a red pool for the cult, iron-grey standing stones for the
constructs.

**CR 11-20 for the Deeps (§8.4a).** `data/bestiary.json` had four entries at CR
10+, and the Deeps' factions topped out far below their band. It now has 26
more, every SRD statblock at CR 11-20 whose type is a Deeps home faction: the
behir, gynosphinx, remorhaz, roc, purple worm and androsphinx (monstrosity);
djinni and efreeti (elemental); storm giant and its rock-throwing archer;
vampire and mummy lord (undead); iron golem (construct); the dragon turtle; ten
adult dragons and the ancient white and brass. The SRD has no fey above CR 3,
so fey got none. No script was committed with the original 316, so
`tools/import_srd_monsters.py` writes the derivation down from data/SCHEMA.md
and proves it: `--check` rebuilds 312 of the 313 original entries the SRD still
has field for field (the giant elk's damage was corrected by hand). The hand
work — faction, habitat, at most three feature templates, the `_notes` of what
was flattened away — is a table in the script. `tests/test_bestiary.gd` now
puts every CR 10+ entry on a board against the level-15 preset party and
autoplays it: each one takes the board, gets a turn, can be hurt and fights
back. Most have no 3D figure and draw in the vector tier, which the test also
pins (`Figures3D.model_path_for` returns "" or a real file).

What they do to a fight, measured: very little yet. Over 200 pinned seeds a
level 15 party's easy roster fields a CR 11+ creature 6 times, a level 20
party's hard roster 20 times; the budget buys bodies first (`Scaler.MAX_FOES`)
and spends the rest on four to eight smaller things. The bench is there for
the Deeps balance work the audit put after it.

**Bands for fey, elementals and constructs (§8.4b).** `core/world_bands.gd`
gains `fey-host`, `elemental-storm` and `construct-column`, weight 2 each (a
kind's weight is its share of the whole map's population, and these live on
its outer quarter only). A monster kind now picks one of its faction's
COUNTRIES and scatters anywhere in it, so a kind the frontier shares with the
Far Deeps is not sent out twice as often because the Deeps became two bands.

**The Far Deeps, split (§5.4).** The Far Deeps are two bands: the inner Deeps,
levels 10-14, and past them **the Unmapped**, levels 15-20 — "Past anywhere
with a name. The maps stop here because the people drawing them did." The
seam sits at the equal-area point of the old ring (0.94 of the extent), each
half an eighth of the map, and meets without overlapping for the reason the
frontier's seam gives. It is an ADDED band, not a rename: `unmapped` carries
`"part_of": "deeps"`, the Far Deeps are still one country of four
(`Regions.countries()`, `country_of()`), and "deeps" still names all of it
wherever something asks a yes-or-no question — a pack's `{"region": "deeps"}`
(`Regions.within`), a scout job sent to the deeps, a trait "in the deeps",
a road event listed for the deeps, the regions_4 achievement (it counts
countries, so crossing a seam inside the Far Deeps is not a fifth region). The
Unmapped plays the Deeps' music. What reads the precise band is what should:
the level clamp, the bar, the crossing card, the caster tier. Every old save
position maps to a band as before. `unmapped` is documented in
docs/modding.md and in tests/fixtures/mod_api.json's `world.bands`.

Measured with `tests/sweep_regions.gd` (now with `CELLS=` to run a subset, and
five new cells), 200 seeds a cell, every row, tier easy, fight seed pinned, back
to back on master (a2faa01) and the branch — the table is in
`core/regions.gd`'s header. The split cannot move a row (a cell is a party and
content level pair); it moves which row a place IS. A level 10 party at the far
edge was in band (96.0%) and is now one band out (content 15, x1.47): 71.5%. A
level 12 party there goes 99.0% -> 84.5%; a level 15 party in the inner Deeps
goes from in band (94.5%) to a pinned level-14 fight, 97.5%. The branch column
differs from master by the new statblocks alone, inside noise on every row
(largest move 3.5 points, 1.3 SE). NO KNOB MOVED.

What a player sees: ten more lairs on every built map (sixteen on the small
map, fifteen on the large and on a generated one), each a diorama of its
people; fey, elemental and construct bands walking the far country; and, past
a new seam near the map's edge, "You are riding out into the Unmapped —
levels 15-20." Screenshots: docs/shots/far-deeps-cult-lair.png,
docs/shots/far-deeps-unmapped.png, docs/shots/far-deeps-dragon-fight.png
(`tests/shot_far_deeps.gd`).

Three tests moved with the map, none weakened. `tests/test_road_trip.gd` walks
twelve reseeded runs instead of four: the new band kinds reshuffle every seed's
draw, and its four seeds went from 1.0 bands met a leg to 0.1 with the road's
population unchanged (monster bands within 250 units of the road, 40 seeds:
1.20 a map before, 1.15 after); at twelve runs master and the branch both read
0.8 (seeds 1000-1011, back to back). It now takes about 380s of its 900.
`tests/drive_completionist.gd` accepts an approach card as the reason the clock
is held after Leave (this run's night now ends with the hand-placed bandits at
Riverhold's gate). `tests/test_world_visit_pages.gd` gives the inn test a
deeper purse to talk a common room dry: there are more lairs to sell word of.

### Still open

- **The Unmapped is a step, not a wall.** One band out at level 10 wins 71.5% at
  easy where level 3 one band out in the Marches wins 28.0%: level 15 content is
  x1.47 of a level 10 party, level 6 content x2.26 of a level 3 one. Whether the
  outer band should be steeper (a higher floor, or its own tier) is a balance
  call for the Deeps pass, with this sweep re-run.
- **The new statblocks rarely reach a roster.** Body count first means a CR 11+
  creature is 3-10% of Deeps fights. If the Deeps should feel like dragons, that
  is a scaler question (MAX_FOES, BIGGEST_SHARE, a per-band habitat or faction
  weighting), measured, not a data one.
- **No fey above CR 3.** The SRD has none; a fey Deeps roster is still pixies
  and a hag. Authored statblocks would need their own provenance note.
- **The new lairs have no measured win rates of their own.** Their bosses are
  core/site.gd's FACTION_BOSS, measured at level 3 by tests/sweep_faction_boss.gd;
  none has been swept at the level of the country it now stands in. Re-run
  `sweep_site_kin`/`sweep_faction_boss` per band before any lair retune.
- **More raids.** Five of the new lairs stand on settled ground (heartland and
  marches) and run raid clocks like the goblin warren (`core/raids.gd`). A town
  holds one raider, so pressure is capped at one per town, but the small map now
  has seven lairs that can raid four towns. Watch it in play.
- **Faction kits are palettes on five shapes.** Authored dioramas (or Meshy
  GLBs with PROVENANCE.md) for the ten new lairs are art work, not done here.
- **Pack maps get nothing.** A pack's lairs are its own; the three bundled packs
  still hold goblin, undead, dragon and one construct lair. A pack author who
  wants the cult's voice places a cultist lair.
- **The turn bar's short names.** A combatant's short name is its first word,
  so an adult red dragon reads "Adult" in the initiative bar (a young one
  already read "Young"); the dragon fight screenshot shows it.
- **CLAUDE.md** still says the bestiary has 316 entries (it has 342).
