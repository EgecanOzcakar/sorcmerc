## The road home — a gentler walk out of a site, and Heartland lairs a level-3 company can clear (2026-09-25)

The owner answered "do all" to the two balance questions the measured pass
(docs/plan/2026-09-25-measured-pass.md, Still open) left for them: the walk
home from a lair, and how hard the Heartland's lairs should be. Both follow up
the design audit (docs/audit-game-design.md §3.2, flattening the wounds curve,
and §7.3, the site knobs that had no sweep). Both were answered with the
lever the measured pass named, and neither touches the wounds curve or the
global site knobs. Every sweep here pins the fight seed and was run back to
back against a copy of master (2662712).

### The walk home (world_threat.gd, world_road_home.gd)

The flattened wounds curve (HURT_AT 0.50, floor x0.72) made pressing on hurt
a real risk, which it was meant to do. It also caught the one walk a player
cannot skip: from a site, where the day was spent on one set of resources,
to the nearest bed. A level-8 company at half HP with no slots won 47.5% of
those road fights (46.0% re-run today). The owner kept the curve. What
changed is how the road reads a company **fresh out of a site**.

- **`core/world_road_home.gd`** (new) owns the clock. `set_out(world)` runs
  when a delve ends cleared or withdrawn (`scenes/world/world.gd`
  `_on_site_done`). A wipe does not start it: defeat's landing has already
  carried the company in and cost it the day. The clock stops at the next
  06:00 (`DAWN_HOUR`) or when the company's next long rest ends, whichever
  comes first. It reads the world clock and `Party.last_long_rest_at` and
  nothing else, so no rest path needed a hook, and nothing is seeded or rolled.
  The one piece of state, `World.walk_home_from`, is in the world save; an
  old save has no key and loads as nobody walking home.
- **`core/world_threat.gd`** owns the number. While the clock runs,
  `assess(party, world)` reads the company's wounds on a curve with two
  knobs of its own, `WALK_HOME_HURT_AT` 0.90 and `WALK_HOME_FLOOR` 0.50
  (x0.45 at zero HP). A `min()` keeps it from ever being harsher than the
  road's curve. `slot_hold` is untouched, so a spent slot still never buys an
  easier fight. The walk reads wounds only, and at full HP both curves give
  the same fight.

`tests/sweep_wounds.gd PART=home`, every slot spent, 200 seeds a cell. First
the candidate floors at level 8 (the 0.39 column is the pre-retune floor):

| hp | floor 0.39 | floor 0.50 | floor 0.60 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 70% | 80.0 | 76.5 | 77.0 |
| 50% | 85.0 | **79.0** | 71.5 |
| 30% | 87.5 | 80.5 | 67.0 |

Then the shipped constants: the road and the walk home side by side, both
through `WorldThreat.assess` with the clock off and then on:

| hp | L3 road | L3 walk home | L8 road | L8 walk home |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | 95.0 | 95.0 | 79.0 | 79.0 |
| 70% | 85.0 | 87.5 | 59.0 | 76.5 |
| 50% | 73.0 | 91.0 | **46.0** | **79.0** |
| 30% | 63.0 | 90.0 | 37.5 | 80.5 |

The target was a level-8 company at half HP with no slots winning at least
75% of road fights on the walk home (the "hard" line). It measures 79.0%. The
same company not fresh from a site measures 46.0%, the same as master run
back to back (road column; master's curve sweep also reads 46.0%). Level 3
comes out gentler than level 8 because its `slot_hold` is smaller, and the
target was set on the harder of the two.

**What a player sees.** The lair's exit message gets one more sentence:
*"Until dawn or a night's sleep, the road home goes easier on their
wounds."* While the clock runs, the map's top bar reads *"· the road home,
until dawn"* beside the country, and that goes as soon as dawn or a bed
ends the walk. Screenshot: `docs/shots/road-home-exit.png`
(`tests/shot_road_home.gd`).

### Heartland lairs (site.gd)

The measured pass had a level-3 company in its own country clearing a lair
18.1% of the time when it took every rest offered (17.6% re-run). The
owner's target was 35–45%, with a level-5 company staying under about 80%.
A rest taken was already measured as the lever, worth about 25 points of
clear rate.

**`Site.BAND_SUPPORT`** gives a band its own support pair, keyed by
`Regions` band id. Only the Heartland has one: support 0.60, rest share 0.70
(the global pair is 0.45 / 0.40). `_build` lays out a lair's floors on the
pair for the band the lair stands in (`Site.band_for`). `pens_ahead` takes
the world too, and `core/quest_posting.gd` passes it, so a rescue is posted
about the interior `for_lair` will actually build. A band with no entry
takes the global pair. A lair outside the Heartland is therefore laid out
exactly as before, draw for draw (`test_site` checks this), and no other
band's measured number moves. This had to be per band: a global change
would have moved every band.

`tests/sweep_site_knobs.gd POLICY=rest`, 210 delves a row:

| | cleared | wiped | rests a delve | reached boss | boss won |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| level 3, master | 17.6% | 82.4% | 0.60 | 41.0% | 43.0% |
| level 3, Heartland pair | **40.0%** | 60.0% | 1.39 | 74.3% | 53.8% |
| level 5, master | 54.3% | 45.7% | 0.70 | 91.0% | 59.7% |
| level 5, Heartland pair | **70.5%** | 29.5% | 1.50 | 98.1% | 71.8% |

**What else could move.** `sweep_faction_boss` fights only the boss room.
That room is not drawn from the site's rng, and its seed is the rng's
starting seed, so it cannot move and was not re-run. `sweep_site_kin`
(level 3, 14 seeds a faction) always takes the first way on, which is always
a fight. There the new pair only reshuffles which combat rooms are drawn:

- rooms won per delve: 1.84 on master, 1.81 on the branch;
- clears across the 15 factions: 8.6% on master, 9.0% on the branch;
- the peoples each lair's rooms draw from are identical.

That is noise. Nothing else was retuned.

### Still open

- **The walk home is generous at level 3** (91.0% at half HP, no slots).
  The target was set at level 8, where the slot hold is largest. If
  low-level walks home read as free, a per-level floor is the next cut, and
  it needs its own sweep.
- **Dawn is a fixed 06:00.** A company that comes out at 05:50 has ten
  minutes of walk home. That follows the rule as written ("until the next
  dawn"). If it reads as a cheat in play, a minimum length is the fix.
- **Only the Heartland has its own support pair.** The Marches and beyond
  keep the global pair and the measured pass's numbers. Whether their lairs
  should also be easier is a separate call, and the per-band table is where
  that call would go.
- **The autopilot still never withdraws**, so these clear rates are a
  careful player's floor rather than their rate.
